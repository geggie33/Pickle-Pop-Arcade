Pickle Pop Arcade - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pickle Pop Arcade.

Information We Collect

Pickle Pop Arcade may process information necessary to operate the game, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and improve the app. Apple and our subscription service providers may process purchase and subscription information according to their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Payments for Pickle Pop Premium made through the iOS app are processed by Apple. Pickle Pop Arcade does not receive your full payment card information.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide game features, verify subscription access, restore purchases, troubleshoot problems, and improve Pickle Pop Arcade.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the app and its subscription features.

Children

Pickle Pop Arcade is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Pickle Pop Arcade develops or its features change.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed through the support contact provided with Pickle Pop Arcade.
